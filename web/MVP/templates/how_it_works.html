How It Works

The Igede Language Data Collection Platform gathers sentences, recordings and images from native speakers and learners, so the language can be preserved and used to build tools for the future.

1

Contribute

Submit text, audio or an image with the right details: direction, category and source.

2

Review

Our team checks every contribution for accuracy and quality.

3

Approved

Verified data becomes part of the public dataset and appears on Explore Data.

What you can contribute

Text

Sentences and translations between English and Igede.

Audio

Recordings and speech: record in your browser or upload a file.

Images

Photos with Igede and English labels for objects, places and culture.

Guidelines

  • Only share content you have the right to share.
  • Use natural, correct Igede, and pick the closest category.
  • Record in a quiet place and speak clearly.
  • Do not include private or sensitive personal information.
